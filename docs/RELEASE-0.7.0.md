# Release 0.7.0 — introductory Quantum II

Quantum II supplies 70 independently reviewed micro-lessons and 67 numerical practice parts. The finite scope covers state representations, ideal projective measurements, operator transformations, dynamics, spin, two-level models, coherent oscillators, tensor products, density operators, entanglement, angular addition and few-particle exchange. Local bridges teach trace, positivity, partial trace, half-angles, exponential/factorial series and rotating-frame terms before use. It is not all quantum physics or a university/laboratory credential; completion marks do not establish independent mastery.

The app now contains 358 actual lessons, 308 numerical practice parts, 31 interactive model types and 99 optional glossaries. Four new analytic models show spin measurement probabilities, exact constant-Hamiltonian transfer, a real-plane density-state disk and local/joint probabilities for a pure composite family. Captions distinguish mathematical state coordinates from spatial pictures, energy detuning from angular-rate detuning, preparation changes from distant outcome control and coherent local conditioning from unrecorded measurement.
