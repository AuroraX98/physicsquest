# Release 0.6.0 — introductory Quantum I

Quantum I now supplies 72 independently reviewed micro-lessons and 60 numerical practice parts. The finite scope is single-particle nonrelativistic wave mechanics: evidence, amplitudes/probabilities, normalization, coherent states, evolution/current, operators and domains, uncertainty, stationary boundaries, hard and finite confinement, flux-aware steps/barriers, the harmonic oscillator, Gaussian packets, spherical/radial coordinates, orbital angular momentum and low hydrogen states. Numerical shooting and its bisection/refinement checks are taught locally. This is not all quantum physics, a complete university/laboratory sequence or a credential.

The app contains 288 actual lessons and 241 numerical practice parts. Four new original analytic models bring the total to 27 model types: coherent well density, a free Gaussian distribution, finite-barrier transmission and hydrogen radial density. Captions distinguish probability density from particle paths, scaled quantities from SI quantities, phase comparisons from time evolution and radial density from volume density. All 216 earlier lesson objects are byte-identical to the previous published source. Existing browser-local progress/export/import schema is unchanged; it is not cloud synchronization.
